   - Primary keys, foreign key cascades, tenant indexes, and unique constraints.

2. **Drizzle Config & Migrations:**
   - `packages/db/drizzle.config.ts`: Drizzle Kit configuration.
   - `packages/db/src/migrations/0000_premium_firedrake.sql`: 15KB pure SQL migration.

3. **State Machine (`packages/db/src/statemachine.ts`):**
   - Pure state transition validator `isValidTransition` and `applyIncidentTransition`.
   - Enforces valid flow: `open → acknowledged → investigating → mitigated → resolved`, plus regression reopen `resolved → open`.
   - Rejecting illegal jumps (e.g. `resolved → acknowledged` or `open → mitigated`).

4. **Transactional Outbox & Inbox (`packages/db/src/outbox.ts`, `inbox.ts`):**
   - Outbox leasing with lease expirations and retry limits.
   - Inbox deduplication keying on `organizationId:eventId`.

5. **Synthetic Seed Generator (`packages/db/src/seeds/seed.ts`):**
   - Generates exactly 10 services, 30 deployments, 100 alerts, and 20 incidents with complete relational integrity.
